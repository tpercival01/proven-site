Privacy Policy

PROVEN is designed to keep your accountability data on your device.

PROVEN does not require an account and does not sell your personal data.

Data stored

PROVEN stores the information needed to run your accountability system locally on your device.

This may include your weekly Standard, completed Proof, session types, active commitments, Recovery Weeks and related progression state.

This data is used to provide PROVEN's core features.

Data sharing

PROVEN does not transmit your accountability data to us or to third-party analytics or advertising services.

When you choose to share a Proof image, Apple's system share sheet lets you choose where that image is sent. That sharing only occurs when you initiate it.

Your control

You can delete your training history from Settings.

You can also reset PROVEN to delete the app's stored accountability data and return to onboarding.

Deleting the app from your device also removes its locally stored app data.

Changes

If PROVEN's data practices change in a future version, this policy will be updated to reflect those changes.
